Withdrawal App / Withdrawal Management Assistant

A Progressive Web App (PWA) for clinical decision support, designed to assist healthcare professionals in managing alcohol withdrawal. 

This tool provides quick access to concise decision trees and common clinical calculators in a fast, offline-capable, and installable web app. Some information has been altered to suit the local context and extend on information available from reputable guidelines.

Intended purpose and disclaimer
This site is intended solely for use by appropriately qualified health professionals, as a clinical reference and decision-support aid. It is not intended for use by patients, carers or members of the public.
It supports clinical judgement and does not replace it. Every recommendation, calculation and pathway it produces is to be independently reviewed by the treating clinician, against the current source guideline and the patient in front of them, before it is acted on. It does not diagnose, it does not direct treatment, and it does not analyse medical images or signals. All content assumes that patients are adults.
This app is an informal quality improvement project based on guidelines and operating procedures from the Western NSW Local Health District, and no section of it has completed independent clinical review. It is not produced, authorised or endorsed by NSW Health or by any other organisation named in it, and where anything here disagrees with the source guideline it cites, the source guideline governs.

Features:
Triage Flowchart: An interactive decision tree for alcohol withdrawal triage.
Clinical Guidelines: Quick reference for inpatient and ambulatory alcohol withdrawal protocols based on local operating protocols in Western NSW LHD.
Calculators & Scales: A suite of essential clinical calculators, including:
CIWA-Ar (Alcohol)
AWS (Alcohol)
COWS (Opiate)
SAWS (Short Alcohol)
CIWA-B (Benzodiazepine)
Cannabis Withdrawal Checklist
Standard Drinks Calculator
PWA Functionality: Fully installable on any compatible device (desktop or mobile) for a native-app feel and complete offline access.

Follow the browser's prompt to "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
Launch the app from the newly created icon on your device's home screen or desktop.
